Common questions

Before you book

Do I need a broker if I already have a bank?

Not always — but a mortgage agent can compare options across lenders through the brokerage, which a single bank branch usually cannot. The right fit depends on your credit, income, property, and goals.

Is the first meeting a sales pitch?

The first conversation is about what the mortgage needs to do. You leave with clearer next steps, whether or not you apply that day.
