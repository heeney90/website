How it works

One relationship. The right lender for the file.

As a Mortgage Agent Level 2 with MPFG, Mark shops suitable lenders rather than locking you into a single bank product — and you work with him directly.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Share your goals, timeline, and what the mortgage needs to do. We clarify what lenders will ask for.

  2. 02

    Structure

    We review income, credit, and property details so the file is positioned clearly before it goes out.

  3. 03

    Lender match

    Through MPFG, your file is shopped to lenders suited to the purchase, refinance, or equity plan.

  4. 04

    Close

    Support through conditions, commitment, and funding so you know what happens next.
